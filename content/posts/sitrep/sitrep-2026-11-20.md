Thursday's session turned a slow decline into a rout. The S&P 500 fell 147.80 points, or 2.02 percent, to 7,175.83; the Nasdaq Composite lost 550.686 points, or 2.25 percent, to 23,879.199; and the Dow Jones Industrial Average dropped 1,101.11 points, or 2.19 percent, to 49,097.06. By Friday morning the VIX had risen to 29.87, and gold had fallen 2.75 percent to $3,584.30 an ounce even as the ten-year Treasury eased to 4.98 percent, a combination that reads less like a flight to safety than like investors raising cash. Overnight, before the opening bell, Russia struck three bridges in Kyiv with missiles and drones, which its defense ministry described as retaliation for Ukraine's attack on the Kerch Bridge.

## The administration and the rule of law

The Federal Register published 209 documents for November 19 and 20: thirty-one rules, thirteen proposed rules and 165 notices, with no presidential document in the window. The empty presidential column is a finding about these two days, not a gap in the record. The November 19 batch was carried in Thursday's edition, so this section concentrates on what appeared on Friday.

The Office of Personnel Management published four documents on Friday. The lead one, [Updates and Amendments to the Civil Service Rules](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/11/20/2026-23732/updates-and-amendments-to-the-civil-service-rules), confirms the effective date of a direct final rule that updated and amended obsolete provisions of the Civil Service Rules; OPM received no significant adverse comments and confirmed the rule effective October 13, 2026. Three companion rules followed: [Procedures for Settling Claims](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/11/20/2026-23731/procedures-for-settling-claims), [FLSA Claims and Compliance](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/11/20/2026-23730/flsa-claims-and-compliance), and [Federal Employees' Retirement System; Present Value Conversion Factors for Spouses of Deceased Separated Employees](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/11/20/2026-23729/federal-employees-retirement-system-present-value-conversion-factors-for-spouses-of-deceased).

The Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation proposed a rule on [custodial deposits](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/11/20/2026-23669/custodial-deposits-implementing-the-21st-century-road-to-housing-act) that would revise its rules for brokered deposits and carry out section 901 of the 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act; comments close January 19, 2027. Homeland Security extended [import restrictions on archaeological and ethnological material of Greece](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/11/20/2026-23724/extension-of-import-restrictions-imposed-on-archaeological-and-ethnological-material-of-greece) and published an [implementation rule for the HAVANA Act of 2021](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/11/20/2026-23713/implementation-of-havana-act-of-2021). Interior proposed listing the [White Sands pupfish](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/11/20/2026-23711/endangered-and-threatened-wildlife-and-plants-threatened-species-status-with-section-4d-rule-for) and the [Mazama newt](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/11/20/2026-23706/endangered-and-threatened-wildlife-and-plants-endangered-species-status-for-mazama-newt) under the Endangered Species Act. Among the notices, the Defense Department filed ten separate arms sales notifications.

## Markets

Thursday's close was the sharpest move of the week. The S&P 500 fell 147.80 points, or 2.02 percent, to 7,175.83; the Nasdaq Composite fell 550.686 points, or 2.25 percent, to 23,879.199; and the Dow fell 1,101.11 points, or 2.19 percent, to 49,097.06.

The overnight tape did not steady. At 6:59 eastern the VIX stood at 29.87, up 1.43 points or 5.03 percent. The ten-year yielded 4.98 percent at 7:00, down about seven basis points. Gold was $3,584.30 an ounce at 6:50, down 2.75 percent, and WTI crude was $82.40 a barrel at 6:11, down 0.97 percent. The dollar index was 103.901 at 7:00, flat. Gold falling alongside equities is the mark of liquidation rather than a haven bid, even as yields eased, though one morning's prints do not establish the read.

Treasury's curve for November 19 ran 4.76 percent at two years, 5.04 at ten and 5.40 at thirty, a 2s10s spread of +0.28, a basis point wider than the day before. Total public debt outstanding was $40,690,769,638,850 on November 18, down $24,176,677,723 from the prior business day. Of that, $32,886,805,439,265 was held by the public and $7,803,964,199,585 was intragovernmental.

## The economy

No new release has landed since the October figures. The all-items consumer price index was 337.511 for October, up from 336.940 in September and 334.980 in August, a monthly rise of about 0.17 percent. Unemployment was 4.1 percent in October, a tenth below September and level with August. Total nonfarm payrolls were 159,293 thousand, 165 thousand above September and 225 thousand above August. Prices rose slowly, hiring held, and the three readings are still one month's, not a trend.

## Elections and the midterms

The count is final, closing the Senate at 56-44, and what remains is the market's read. On Polymarket the next Senate majority leader drew the most trade of any midterm question, with Chuck Schumer at 94.5 percent on $18,173 of twenty-four-hour volume and Chris Van Hollen at 5.3 percent. The same venue priced a Democratic House popular-vote margin of 14 to 16 points at 92.7 percent, NC-05 for Chuck Hubbard at 99.1 percent, and an Ohio governor's margin for Acton of 6 to 9 points at 99.3 percent. Those are prices, not calls.

## The world

Russia answered Ukraine's strike on the Kerch Bridge with a barrage across the country overnight, and Kyiv took the worst of it. [Al Jazeera reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/20/russia-unleashes-retaliatory-barrage-on-kyiv-bridges) that missiles and drones damaged Kyiv's Podilskyi, Darnitskyi and Southern bridges over the Dnipro, hit energy infrastructure, and struck a hospital in the Zaporizhia region; nuclear plants cut output, leaving outages in Kyiv and ten regions. Prime Minister Sergii Koretskyi put the toll at eight dead, six of them in Kyiv, and twenty-eight wounded. Russia's defense ministry said it targeted bridges used to move weapons and military equipment. Putin had promised the response: "We will respond to these attacks, and I want everyone to be clear about that."

The strike landed hours after the week's diplomacy. At a Berlin meeting of Ukraine's European allies on Thursday, Trump's envoy Jared Kushner said Russia had come around to an Article 5-style security guarantee for postwar Ukraine, telling the gathering, "I think we've gotten the Russians comfortable with that concept," [Al Jazeera reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/20/kushner-says-russia-ok-with-nato-like-security-pact-for-ukraine-is-it). The same account noted the plan's vagueness: Putin has called Ukrainian NATO membership unacceptable, Deputy Foreign Minister Sergei Ryabkov rejected any arrangement that amounted to quasi membership, and Zelenskyy said the allies had no shared understanding of what the guarantees would mean in practice.
