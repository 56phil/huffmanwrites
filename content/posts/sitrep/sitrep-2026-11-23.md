Ukrainian naval drones struck two Russian vessels off Libya on Sunday and sank one of them, the corvette Merkury, at its berth in the Benghazi naval base. Ukraine's security service, the SBU, said it launched seven domestically built drones from a mothership, and Volodymyr Zelenskyy called the operation "exemplary" and said the second vessel, the dry cargo ship MV Angara, was carrying weapons for Russia. The strike landed some 2,400 kilometres from the front line, a week after Ukrainian attacks on the Kerch bridge and on the Baltic port of Kaliningrad, and it put a Russian warship at the bottom of a harbour held by a Libyan government that Moscow backs.

The United Nations mission in Libya expressed "serious concern" over the attacks and called for "an immediate, independent and transparent investigation". Libya, it said on X, "cannot afford, and must not become a battleground for foreign conflicts." There was no immediate comment from Russia.

The markets, by contrast, entered the week calmer than they left Friday. The S&P 500 has no Monday print yet; its last reading is Friday's close at 6,910.39, down 3.70 percent, with the Dow at 48,147.33, down 1.93 percent. In the pre-market quotes this morning the CBOE volatility index had fallen 15.85 percent to 30.20 by 06:59 CT, and gold had risen 3.02 percent to $3,621.60 an ounce by 06:50 CT. A falling volatility index and a rising gold price point in different directions, and the report does not force them into one story: what is clear is that the panic of Friday's close is not being repeated at this hour.

In Washington the record was thin and administrative. The Federal Register carried no presidential document, but five rules, seven proposed rules and seventy-nine notices, and the courts produced one new complaint and nothing else.

## The administration and the rule of law

The day's one consequential document is a labor rule. The Labor Department's Wage and Hour Division published a final rule rescinding its current test for deciding who is an employee and who is an independent contractor under the Fair Labor Standards Act, and replacing it with an analysis "similar to what it published in a final rule promulgated in 2021, with several modifications and enhancements". The rule also amends the regulations under the Family and Medical Leave Act and the Migrant and Seasonal Agricultural Worker Protection Act, so that the same test governs all three statutes, on the Department's reasoning that both laws incorporate the labor act's scope of employment.

It is [the Department's final rule on employee and independent contractor status](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/11/23/2026-23777/employee-or-independent-contractor-status-under-the-fair-labor-standards-act-family-and-medical), 91 FR 75646, Docket No. WHD-2026-0001, and the register flags it as significant. It takes effect on January 22, 2027, which means the classification questions it governs, gig work, franchise arrangements, and the whole gray economy of people paid as contractors while working under direction, will be decided under the old analysis through the winter and under the new one after.

The other four rules were narrow: an Agriculture Department rule extending the postponement of the horse protection regulations, a Homeland Security extension of import restrictions on Egyptian archaeological material, a technical amendment to transportation rules, and an airspace amendment at Del Rio, Texas. The seven proposed rules were two airspace changes, two airworthiness directives for ATR regional aircraft, a Privacy Act rule for the Occupational Safety and Health Review Commission, a Connect America Fund item, and a labeling scheme for internet-connected devices. The seventy-nine notices were the usual commerce of foreign-trade zones, information collections, advisory committee meetings, sanctions listings, and trade reviews.

## The economy

The Bureau of Labor Statistics' newest readings are all for October 2026, and they describe an economy that is not contracting. The consumer price index stood at 337.511, up 0.17 percent on September's 336.940 after September's own 0.59 percent rise, so the monthly pace of price increases slowed by more than half. Unemployment was 4.1 percent, a tenth below September's 4.2 and level with August. Nonfarm payrolls reached 159,293 thousand, a gain of 165,000 on the month, after a 60,000 gain in September.

Taken together, the three say prices cooled, hiring strengthened, and the jobless rate held near four percent. That is a steady picture, and it is a month old. The equity market sold off on Friday on expectations about what comes next, not on October's data, and the gap between the two is where the week's argument sits.

## The world

The SBU's account, [reported by The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/23/ukraine-naval-drones-strike-russian-warship-libya), said the Merkury had docked at Benghazi to refuel and take on supplies under a cooperation agreement between Russia and the eastern Libyan government, and that the operation showed "the Russian military fleet and its logistics networks have no safe routes, even far beyond the Black Sea." Zelenskyy released video footage, filmed by an approaching drone, showing the drones as they smashed into the side of the warship at its berth. Benghazi residents heard explosions and saw flames at the base, and civil defence units put out the fire, according to eastern Libyan authorities.

The Merkury was not an anonymous hull. Vladimir Putin toured the St Petersburg yard where it was built in 2019 and backed reviving a tsarist-era brig's name for it; at the 2023 Navy Day parade he singled it out among thirty new vessels as evidence Russia was "consistently building up the power of its fleet," and called its St George flag "a sign of the inviolability of our naval traditions." Russian state media called it the newest missile corvette and claimed its stealth made it invisible to radar. It was sunk at its berth.

Moscow has used eastern Libya as a staging post for its presence in North Africa and the Sahel, backing Khalifa Haftar's government in Benghazi. Ukraine has been working the same ground: the Associated Press reported in April that Ukrainian drone specialists had been operating at military sites in Misrata, Tripoli and Zawiya, and Libyan authorities denied a CNN report in September that they had allowed Ukraine a base from which to strike Russia's shadow fleet in the Mediterranean. Sunday's operation is one Ukraine has now openly claimed.

Elsewhere the prediction markets marked a quieter weekend. Polymarket puts the Israel-Iran ceasefire continuing through November 30 at 92.5 percent on $142,928 of twenty-four-hour volume, and through December 31 at 69.5 percent on $88,152, a decay that prices the odds of the truce holding through the year at roughly seven in ten.
