A situation report, published every morning.

Each edition is built the same way. A collector fetches the day's hard record
first — the documents the Federal Register published, the opinions and filings
the federal courts entered, the Treasury's own yield curve, the federal debt to
the penny, the labor and price data the Bureau of Labor Statistics publishes,
and the implied odds on the prediction markets with enough volume to be worth
reading. Everything else — the news that has no feed — is fetched and cited
rather than recalled.

The numbers come from the sources themselves. Where a source says nothing, the
report says so.

This section is deliberately kept out of the home page's Recent Posts feed: a
daily series would otherwise occupy every slot in it. The current edition is
shown on the home page instead.
