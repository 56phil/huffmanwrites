The United States and Iran described two different waterways on Wednesday. United States Central Command [said in a post on X](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202610071420) that traffic was crossing the Strait of Hormuz carrying commercial goods and energy supplies, "including 20 million barrels of crude oil," and that "the United States and regional partners clearly control the strait." Mohammad Reza Naghdi, a senior adviser to the Revolutionary Guards commander-in-chief, said the same day that the strait "is closed, and the armed forces have full control over it," and that it would stay closed until Iran's demands were satisfied. Both statements carry the date October 7, and they cannot both describe the same water. Crude was up on the day: the front month for West Texas Intermediate stood at $89.10, up 0.93 percent, as of 8:31 p.m. Eastern.

## The administration and the rule of law

The Federal Register published 167 documents across the two-day window: one presidential document, 17 rules, 14 proposed rules, and 135 notices. The sole presidential document is a proclamation of [National Manufacturing Day, 2026](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/07/2026-20629/national-manufacturing-day-2026).

The documents that matter cluster on crypto. FinCEN [withdrew](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/06/2026-20430/requirements-for-certain-transactions-involving-convertible-virtual-currency-or-digital-assets) the notice of proposed rulemaking that would have required banks and money services businesses to report, keep records, and verify the identity of customers in transactions involving convertible virtual currency or digital assets held in unhosted wallets. Its abstract states that "FinCEN will not take any further action on this NPRM." FinCEN separately [withdrew](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/06/2026-20429/proposal-of-special-measure-regarding-convertible-virtual-currency-mixing-as-a-class-of-transactions) its finding under section 311 of the USA PATRIOT Act that international convertible virtual currency mixing is a class of transactions of primary money laundering concern, together with the special measure that finding proposed.

Against the withdrawals, the SEC [proposed](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/06/2026-20466/adviser-and-regulated-fund-custody-rules-crypto-custody-rules) new custody rules under the Investment Company Act and amendments to the custody rule under the Advisers Act, "to address how regulated investment companies may custody crypto securities and similar investments" and "how registered investment advisers may custody client crypto funds and securities." The proposal also modernizes the existing custody rules and adds questions to Form ADV and Form N-CEN about tokenized private funds. The SEC also [granted approval](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/07/2026-20507/self-regulatory-organizations-cboe-bzx-exchange-inc-order-granting-approval-of-a-proposed-rule) for Cboe BZX to list and trade 3x leveraged exchange-traded funds tracking gold, silver, bitcoin, ether, crude oil, and natural gas, each a series of the VS Trust. Set side by side, the two withdrawals remove proposed controls while the SEC's custody proposal and the listing order build a framework in their place.

Three other items carry. The Trade Representative [continued](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/07/2026-20510/continuation-of-actions-chinas-acts-policies-and-practices-related-to-technology-transfer) both Section 301 actions on China after receiving requests for continuation from domestic industries, so the actions "have not terminated and will remain in effect," subject to the statutory four-year review; a [companion notice](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/07/2026-20511/notice-of-conforming-amendment-to-product-exclusion-chinas-acts-policies-and-practices-related-to) made a conforming amendment to one product exclusion after the International Trade Commission changed tariff schedule categories effective July 1, 2026. The Labor Department [withdrew](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/07/2026-20523/statements-of-general-policy-or-interpretation-not-directly-related-to-regulations-withdrawal-of) its proposed rule of July 2, 2025, which would have removed parts of the Code of Federal Regulations that were never issued through notice-and-comment rulemaking, "formally discontinuing this rulemaking process." And the Federal Reserve [extended](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/07/2026-20509/loans-to-executive-officers-directors-and-principal-shareholders-of-member-banks-bank-holding) the comment period on its Regulation O proposal, which would update the rule governing loans by member banks to their insiders, by 30 days to November 4, 2026.

The Environmental Protection Agency also [gave notice](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/06/2026-20468/initial-air-quality-designations-for-the-2024-revised-primary-annual-fine-particle-pm25-national) of its intended approach to area designations under the 2024 primary annual fine particle standard. It notified states, territories, and Tribes of their initial designations on or about October 2, 2026, and opened a comment period before it promulgates final designations.

## The economy

Three series from the Bureau of Labor Statistics, each for the month named, which lags the publication date.

Consumer prices rose in August. The CPI-U all-items index was 334.980, from 333.918 in July and 333.952 in June, so the index was flat through the summer and then rose 1.062 points in a single month. The unemployment rate was 4.2 percent in September, a tenth above the 4.1 percent recorded in both August and July, the first increase among the three months shown. Total nonfarm payrolls were 159,044 thousand in September, 29 thousand above August, which was itself 133 thousand above July.

Taken together, the three say the labor market is still adding jobs and doing so more slowly, with the September gain the smallest of the three months on the page, while price growth reasserted itself in August after a flat July. September prices are not yet published; the August index is the newest the Bureau has released.

## The world

The diplomacy behind the Hormuz dispute, as the reporting available describes it.

Vice President JD Vance asked Iran to accept a "meaningful" cut to its uranium enrichment capacity as a condition of ending the war, and framed the threshold as a question: "If you don't want a nuclear weapon, then why do you need 60 percent enriched fuel?" He added, "If you want to show commitment to not building a nuclear weapon, don't build highly enriched fuel," and called the point "a very basic threshold issue." Talks are under way with Tehran, he said, though "it's not totally clear how their country makes decisions."

Secretary of State Marco Rubio, speaking in Athens, said, "Iran cannot have a nuclear weapon, simple, period, end of story." He asserted that Iran had "lost complete control" of the Strait of Hormuz and that its economy was in "total and complete freefall." President Trump said the war would conclude by "the nice way or the not-so-nice way."

Iran's president, Masoud Pezeshkian, called negotiations "futile," saying that "every time we come to the negotiating table, war is immediately imposed upon us," and that "negotiations become futile, and our nation will not accept such an approach." Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi told foreign ambassadors in Tehran on October 4 that only "negotiations based on justice and fairness" could end the conflict. Two governments thus disagree in public about whether the strait is open, and neither has produced evidence the other accepts.

## Technology and AI

OpenAI published 372 mathematical results to GitHub rather than to journals, [according to reporting at The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/openai-dumps-372-ai-generated-math-proofs-on-github-telling-the-academic-world-to-keep-up/). The results came from an unreleased internal frontier model, most of them produced from a single prompt to a single agent and averaging about three hours of ChatGPT Pro Thinking compute apiece; some required multiple attempts. Many carry Lean formalizations, with more promised. OpenAI says the same model produced a Navier-Stokes solution now under formal review, an effort that used a swarm of 10,000 agents and millions of dollars, which is not how the new batch was made.

The company consulted the Institute for Advanced Study's Advisory Group on Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence and, the report says, only loosely followed its public recommendations: no prompts were released, and compute was disclosed only as an average.

Reaction runs from welcome to alarm. The report describes Timothy Gowers, a Fields Medal winner on the advisory group, warning that mathematical literature could balloon within a decade or two without a human community that genuinely understands it, and Terence Tao arguing that the training of young mathematicians should stress the human side of the discipline and sharply constrain the use of AI tools. An open letter the report attributes to 25 Fields Medal winners is titled "A Severe Misalignment of AI in Mathematics." Lean can verify that a proof is logically correct. It cannot say whether the result matters.

Polymarket [priced a Gemini 4.0 release by October 31](https://gamma-api.polymarket.com/events?slug=gemini-4pt0-released-by-june-30-2026) at 88.5 percent on $26,645 of 24-hour volume, and [gave Google](https://gamma-api.polymarket.com/events?slug=which-company-has-the-best-ai-model-end-of-october) a 68.8 percent chance of holding the best model at the end of October.
