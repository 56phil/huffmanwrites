A weekly report on the Kansas City Chiefs, published every Tuesday evening
during the season.

Each week the report covers the game just played and what decided it, where the
Chiefs sit in the AFC West and the conference seed list, the injury and
personnel news with dates, the next opponent and how to watch, and what the
season looks like from here.

The reporting is built from the league's own data feeds and from the coverage
the piece cites. Numbers come from the feed, and every claim carries a source.
